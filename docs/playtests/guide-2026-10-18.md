# Wildcard playtest: retired mountain guide persona (Sonnet subagent), 2026-10-18

The tester played the live site at 360×690 without reading the source. This is a simulated persona, not a human.

**Rings true.**
- Refusals come as "not now, after I drink" rather than flat no's, and the journal records each one.
- Pushing costs trust. "Fine. Since you insist." Later Tamar said: "Last time you sent me: climb the ridge: failed. Not again."
- Earshot matters: walking up to a person to tell them works where shouting fails.
- The turnaround time is "the best thing in the game." The guide blew through 13:00, then held to 16:00: "You turned when you said you would."
- Quiet details work: Tamar checking boots, Bilal turning his tin over, the dusk prayer. "Nobody explains any of it."
- The slips felt real: hurt, not dead, late in the day and close to home.

**Rings false.**
- Nell turned back alone at 09:05. "A guide would never allow a novice to descend alone."
- The party stretches out and the leader trails out of earshot of their own rope team.
- Controls: a selected command stays selected, so later taps resend it. The guide may have urged when he meant to ask.

**Felt.** The long descent with an injured Bilal. "Everyone was back at the hut." "It lets you count, and I did." "For someone who has led people, it is uncomfortably honest."
