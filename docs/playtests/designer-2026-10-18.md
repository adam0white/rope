# Game-design expert review (2026-10-18)

Relayed by the coordinator. Verbatim line: "The bones are good. Right now the game undermines its own two best ideas: that I am the one asking, and that the day gets read back to me truthfully."

Ranked:
1. "You" spoke and insisted without player input in wait-only stretches ("Everyone, on we go. Now."; turnarounds at 13:00 and 16:00). The player must own every utterance.
2. The debrief must match the facts: "Everyone down" with three out; Bilal "turned back at the col" yet "on the top"; "badly hurt" carried nothing into climb 2. The hut voice should fall back when it says too little.
3. Panel and map at 360 px: cancel must close the panel; keep the "you" row visible; spread figures and show who lags; rope-up as pick A, pick B, done; replace template leaks ("climb the ridge: failed"); give the five distinct voices.
Also: seven characters introduced at once; normal speed drags (aim for about 20 minutes a climb); a console error on load.
