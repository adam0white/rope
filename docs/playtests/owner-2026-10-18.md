## What happened

40 inputs over a 05:00–23:00 day. Outcome: Oskar on top at 14:30 and down alone at 22:14; Wren fell three times (14:31, 16:59, 18:54) and was still out at night; the other three down by dusk. The leader kept leading up until 17:59 and turned at 19:45, after everyone else had turned.

The export holds game minutes only, not real time or speed changes. Real-time durations below assume normal speed (48 game minutes per real minute, `src/ui/pace.ts`); this is an assumption, not measured.

## Diagnosis: where it went flat

1. **A slow first hour.** The first input was "lead on" at 05:14. The next was at 06:27, and the first answer that was not a yes came at 06:44: 104 game minutes, about two real minutes of story lines with nothing to decide. No hook ties the opening to a problem the player must solve.
2. **Choices that didn't matter.** Of the 15 asks before noon, every non-yes was a deferral of the form "after I climb the X". People did what was asked a few minutes later. Rest and climb asks made no visible difference to anyone. Ask, urge and insist were never compared, because urge was used once and insist never.
3. **Feedback hidden in the word "here".** Five refusals said "not from here", "Look where we are" or "That can't be done here":
   - Rope-ups at 11:41 and 12:31 failed because the pair was more than 40 m apart. The game never said that distance was the reason.
   - "Eat" at 13:29 failed because the three members had eaten their two rations. The verb was still enabled.
   - The 11:41 rope asked Nell to rope up with Nell, a UI bug, and logged the refusal twice.
   - The player was refused on three tries without learning why.
4. **A storm told, then nothing.** "Cloud is building in the west" at 10:43 drew five plain "heard" answers. Whether the storm (from 12:32) changed anyone's choice was never shown.
5. **The crisis came when the player was away.** From 14:30 the party broke up:
   - Oskar summited alone and asked three times for company.
   - Wren fell badly at 14:31.
   - Bilal and Tamar went down roped with Oskar, but Oskar later went down alone anyway.
   - Meanwhile the player pressed "lead on" ten times between 13:41 and 17:04 and fixed a rope on the summit step at 16:49, after the last person had turned.
   - The fall and the calls for company reached the player as margin lines. The player kept climbing, so nothing in the game made the hurt person urgent.
   - Showing the fall without hurrying the player to act is the biggest gap between cause and effect in this run.
6. **Late taps with no effect.** Between 18:07 and 19:11, "walk to Nell" and "walk to Bilal" each ended in "X was down; you stopped where you were", one minute later. Facts told at 19:20 and 19:32 went to people already at the hut.
7. **A long ending with nothing to do.** After turning at 19:45, there were 195 game minutes until 23:00 with no input. That is about four real minutes at normal speed. The player watched a solo descent while Wren was out and nothing could be done.

In short, the game has real stakes: Wren's night out and Oskar's lone summit. But the player met them late, by accident, and could not tell what caused them. The morning, when the player has the most say, is the part with the least at stake.

## Early consult (game designer persona, Sonnet, source unread)

It read this diagnosis and played the live build at 360×780 from 05:00 to 07:43. Its verdict on its own play: "I got bored almost at once." The only moment it liked was a character line ("Wren came because Oskar said she wouldn't"). Its ranked list:
1. Open with a problem, not a stroll.
2. Make a refusal say why, in the person's voice; grey out verbs that cannot work; fix the Nell-to-Nell rope.
3. Give each person a want that collides with another's.
4. Wake the player when someone falls.
5. Put a crux decision where the player is present.
6. End the day with something to do.

It said to do 2 and 1 first, then 4 and 3.

## Fixes chosen (fun per effort)

1. **A choice at the hut.** The intro page now ends with "when do you turn back?": 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, or "We'll see". The promise input already existed in the sim but was hidden until 10:30. It now comes first, made where everyone hears it, and they hold you to it. This is a UI-only change.
2. **Refusals say why.**
   - An HF refusal for an act that is not on offer (`unavailable`) now names the cause the host knows: "Nell's too far off to tie to." or "My food's gone." The verdict is still HF's.
   - "Eat" reads "no food" and is greyed for someone with no rations.
   - "Walk to X" is greyed once X is down.
   - A rope can no longer pair someone with themself.
3. **The leader says why they stopped, and falls call the leader.**
   - When the leader stops on their own, the note strip says why: "You are waiting for Tamar and Bilal to catch up." (in the owner's run this lasted 13:45–14:55), "The way forks here", "You stopped below the summit step: it could take a fixed rope", or "You are on the top".
   - A hurt of severity 0.15 or more opens a solid rope-coloured bar for 45 game minutes, or until the leader reaches the person. It reads "Wren fell on the last scramble and is hurt", with "go to her", plus "turn her back" when she is in earshot.
   - A fall also drops fast speed back to normal.
   - The sim's party-pace wait is now an exported `waitingFor`, so the game and the message share one rule. The change does not alter behaviour: `npm run measure` is identical before and after.
4. **The end of the day goes quickly.** Once the leader and everyone still out have turned, and nobody is asking or freshly hurt, a bar offers "walk on to nightfall". It runs the remaining minutes with no inputs, so replay stays exact. In the browser, the owner's 21:00 to 23:00 took under 5 seconds.
