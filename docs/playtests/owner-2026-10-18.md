## Diagnosis: where it went flat

1. **A slow first hour.** The first input was "lead on" at 05:14. The next was at 06:27, and the first answer that was not a yes came at 06:44: 104 game minutes, about two real minutes of story lines with nothing to decide. No hook ties the opening to a problem the player must solve.
2. **Choices that didn't matter.** Of the 15 asks before noon, every non-yes was a deferral of the form "after I climb the X". People did what was asked a few minutes later. Rest and climb asks made no visible difference to anyone. Ask, urge and insist were never compared, because urge was used once and insist never.
3. **Feedback hidden in the word "here".** Five refusals said "not from here", "Look where we are" or "That can't be done here":
   - Rope-ups at 11:41 and 12:31 failed because the pair was more than 40 m apart. The game never said that distance was the reason.
   - "Eat" at 13:29 failed because the three members had eaten their two rations. The verb was still enabled.
   - The 11:41 rope asked Nell to rope up with Nell, a UI bug, and logged the refusal twice.
   - The player was refused on three tries without learning why.
4. **A storm told, then nothing.** "Cloud is building in the west" at 10:43 drew five plain "heard" answers. Whether the storm (from 12:32) changed anyone's choice was never shown.
5. **The crisis came when the player was away.** From 14:30 the party broke up:
   - Oskar summited alone and asked three times for company.
   - Wren fell badly at 14:31.
   - Bilal and Tamar went down roped with Oskar, but Oskar later went down alone anyway.
   - Meanwhile the player pressed "lead on" ten times between 13:41 and 17:04 and fixed a rope on the summit step at 16:49, after the last person had turned.
   - The fall and the calls for company reached the player as margin lines. The player kept climbing, so nothing in the game made the hurt person urgent.
   - Showing the fall without hurrying the player to act is the biggest gap between cause and effect in this run.
6. **Late taps with no effect.** Between 18:07 and 19:11, "walk to Nell" and "walk to Bilal" each ended in "X was down; you stopped where you were", one minute later. Facts told at 19:20 and 19:32 went to people already at the hut.
7. **A long ending with nothing to do.** After turning at 19:45, there were 195 game minutes until 23:00 with no input. That is about four real minutes at normal speed. The player watched a solo descent while Wren was out and nothing could be done.

In short, the game has real stakes: Wren's night out and Oskar's lone summit. But the player met them late, by accident, and could not tell what caused them. The morning, when the player has the most say, is the part with the least at stake.
