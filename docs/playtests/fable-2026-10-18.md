# Fable playtest (2026-10-18)

Relayed by the coordinator. Fable may have played a build from between deploys: its footer said HF 2.5.0, and the live bundle checked afterwards says 2.6.0.

Verbatim verdict: "The fiction holds: the journal and the night-at-the-hut lines are worth the whole thing. The simulation under it is doing real work … What is broken is the player's seat."

Findings, in the coordinator's order:
1. Correctness: the end-of-day accounts disagree. Chips said "down", the journal "Everyone was back", the stamp "Some still on the hill". The read-back had Bilal "turned back at the ridge" while the journal put him on top with the tin. "Badly hurt" contradicted "got up hurt".
2. Resuming after the climb ended landed on a dead map, with no read-back and no "On to The Tooth".
3. The player's seat: "lead on" ran the leader ahead of the party; there was no way to stay with them or walk to someone; the tutorial could strand the player; the camera lost the party; far events (slip, untie, summit) were invisible.
4. Refusals came too late: five template yeses to "climb on" is too agreeable. Force prompts such as "we all turn" gave no immediate visible feedback and did not close when resolved.
5. Layout: the "you" row should stay visible above any open prompt; bubbles collided with the tutorial bar and each other; weather doodles ran over text.
6. Minor: rope-up should offer ask/urge/insist; the save is too large.
